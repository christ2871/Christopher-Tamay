## Security Gap Analysis
Weakness Zone: The Server Room
Weakness: The server room does not have a locked door, but there are
no camera or alarm that was mentioned. Therefore this means that the
suspcious activity goes not noticed.
## The Evidence Plan
1. The server logs must be reviewed to see who entered and at what time
2. The camera must be checked to determine if the enterance is montiored
3. The alarm records must be reviewed, to determine if there have been
unathorized access at a certain point

## Compensating Control
The security guard should regularly patrol and document their inspections
everytime they check. This therefore helps detects if any suspicouys
activity is going on or when cameras are missing or an alarm goes off.
